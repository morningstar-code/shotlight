# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-28

First release, for Windows: a free download from the public repository. Shotlight is free to use and closed source.

- Text search over every screenshot with PP-OCRv6 (50+ Latin-script languages, Chinese, Japanese), accent- and case-insensitive, with whole-word ranking and labelled near matches for typos and OCR misreads.
- Extra scripts on demand: Korean, Cyrillic, Arabic, Devanagari, Thai, Greek, Tamil, Telugu.
- Visual search with SigLIP 2 (multilingual) or CLIP, opt-in and local.
- Detected values (one-time codes, links, emails, phones, amounts, IPs, colors, paths) shown as copyable chips.
- Kinds, titles and burst grouping; filters for folder, date, kind and detected values, in English and Spanish.
- Privacy shield: secrets detected, blurred and masked, never given to AI assistants.
- MCP server with five tools.
- Desktop window with a global shortcut, tray icon and no taskbar button (Windows); browser mode everywhere.
- Text selectable directly on the image; drag a box to copy lines.
- Cleanup suggestions and trash with undo; optional capture of clipboard images.
- Interface in English and Spanish, night and paper themes.
